# How to run a web survey

The procedure for investigating a topic systematically on the web. It covers setting the question, the search plan, collecting and judging information, and checking the survey's coverage. Detail lives in `references/`. This file shows the order of work and where to read.

## When to use

- Systematically investigate a specific topic or question with web search and page fetches
- Collect information from several angles and organize the evidence and sources

The artifacts are a note of the question and scope, survey notes by angle, a source list, and open questions.

## Procedure

1. **Set the question and scope.** Follow `references/scope-and-questions.md`. Write the question, the scope, and what is out of scope in a working note.
2. **Build a search plan.** In `references/search-plan.md`, break the question into subtopics and decide the search terms and the order to investigate them.
3. **Collect and record.** Follow `references/web-research.md` and repeat search and page fetches. Record evidence, sources, reliability, and open questions in the survey notes, and update the plan when needed.
4. **Check coverage and evidence.** Use `references/evidence-bar.md` to confirm that the angles the question needs were investigated and that the sources are wide enough and strong enough. If there is a gap or a bias, survey further.
